
=== 1. String: a value under a key
> SET lib:book:1:title Кобзар
OK
> GET lib:book:1:title
Кобзар

=== 2. String with a time limit: a reading room seat is held for 15 minutes
> SET lib:seat:12 reader:5 EX 900 NX
OK
> SET lib:seat:12 reader:6 EX 900 NX
(nil)
> GET lib:seat:12
reader:5
> TTL lib:seat:12
900

=== 3. Counter: views of a book page
> INCR lib:book:1:views
1
> INCR lib:book:1:views
2
> INCRBY lib:book:1:views 10
12
> GET lib:book:1:views
12

=== 5. List: the last books a reader opened, only the 3 most recent are kept
> LPUSH lib:reader:5:recent book:1 book:8 book:3 book:6

> LTRIM lib:reader:5:recent 0 2

> LRANGE lib:reader:5:recent 0 -1

book:3
book:8
